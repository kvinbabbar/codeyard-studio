What's inside

Everything you need to train smarter

From logging your first set to advanced strength analytics — Trainix grows with you.

Log Sets in Seconds

Ghost-text pre-fills your last weight and reps for every exercise. Confirm and log — no typing required.

150+ Exercise Library

Searchable exercises by muscle group, equipment, and movement type — plus your own custom exercises.

Automatic PR Tracking

Trainix detects new personal records automatically — estimated 1RM, max weight, max volume, and max reps.

Structured Training Plans

Activate a program and Trainix guides you day by day, automatically advancing as you complete each session.

Deep Analytics

Track consistency, streaks, and PRs for free — unlock Volume Trend and Strength Progress charts with Pro.

AI Coach PRO

Get GPT-powered coaching personalised to your real training data — programming, nutrition, recovery, and technique.

Nutrition Tracking

Log food from a built-in database, scan barcodes, or add custom foods to track calories and macros.

Habit Tracking

Build consistency beyond the gym — water, sleep, steps, protein, and custom habits with daily streaks.

Health Sync

Automatically export completed workouts, body weight, and body fat percentage to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Privacy first

Your training data stays yours

  • Stored on your device

    Workouts, PRs, plans, and metrics are saved locally using SQLite — not on a server.

  • No account required

    Start logging workouts immediately. No sign-up, no email, no password.

  • Export anytime

    Create a full JSON backup of your training history whenever you want, and restore it on any device.

  • Health sync is optional

    Apple Health and Health Connect integration is entirely opt-in and can be turned off at any time.

Built to keep your data private

No cloud sync, no third-party data sales — your training history belongs to you.
