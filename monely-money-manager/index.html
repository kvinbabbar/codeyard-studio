Features

Everything you need to master your money

A comprehensive suite of personal finance tools built for speed, complete privacy, and total financial control.

Expense & Income Tracking

Quickly record expenses and incomes in seconds. Organize transactions with categories, dates, accounts, and intelligent notes suggested from your history.

Account-to-Account Transfers

Move funds seamlessly between Bank, Cash, and Savings wallets without distorting your income and spending totals. Both account ledgers update instantly.

Multi-Account & Wallet Management

Manage all your bank accounts, physical cash envelopes, credit cards, and digital wallets in one unified dashboard with real-time balance calculations.

Category Budgets & Limits

Set monthly spending limits for individual categories (Groceries, Dining, Shopping, etc.). Live progress indicators alert you before you overspend.

Recurring Bills & Subscriptions

Automate your fixed commitments. Set up recurring expenses, incomes, or transfers with flexible frequencies (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).

Smart Bill Reminders

Never miss a due date again. Monely notifies you ahead of time when recurring bills, subscriptions, or utility payments are due so you avoid late fees.

Smart Insights & Analytics

Understand where your money goes with monthly summaries, category distribution charts, income vs. expense breakdowns, and daily spending trend analysis.

Instant Search & Multi-Filters

Locate any past record in milliseconds. Full-text search combined with multi-criteria filtering by date range, account, category, or transaction amount.

Secure JSON Backup & Restore

You own your data. Export a complete JSON backup file via the OS share sheet to Google Drive, iCloud, Files, or email. Restore on any new device with one tap.

Data Export (CSV & TXT)

Export your transaction history as clean CSV files for Excel, Google Sheets, or Apple Numbers, or generate formatted TXT reports for tax filing and accounting.

Multi-Currency & Global Locale

Monely detects your currency automatically from your device locale, or lets you choose manually from global currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CAD, AUD, etc.).

Dark & Light Mode

Thoughtfully designed for readability day and night. Automatically adapts to your system setting or toggle your preferred appearance manually in settings.

100% Privacy

Your finances are
nobody else's business.

Most personal finance apps require your email, connect to third-party bank aggregators, or store your spending history on cloud servers. Monely was architected from day one on a zero-trust, local-first foundation.

  • No Cloud Sync, No Remote Servers

    All records reside inside a sandboxed SQLite database on your device via expo-sqlite. Nothing is sent to our servers — ever.

  • Zero Personal Financial Tracking

    We never track, inspect, or sell your spending habits. Your financial transactions and account balances remain 100% private to you.

  • No Account or Sign-Up Needed

    Open the app and start logging immediately. No email verification, no passwords, no phone numbers, no friction.

  • 100% Functional Offline

    Every single feature — adding entries, running analytics, setting budgets, exporting data — works with zero internet connection.

Device-Local Sovereignty

Because your financial database never leaves your phone, there are no remote databases to get breached or leaked. Complete privacy, by design.
