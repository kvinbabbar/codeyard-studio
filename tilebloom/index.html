How to Play

Three steps to bloom

Pick up and play in seconds. The challenge unfolds naturally.

1

Tap a Tile

Select any accessible tile from the layered board to move it into your tray.

2

Match Three

Collect three tiles of the same type — they vanish from your tray automatically.

3

Clear the Board

Remove every tile from the board to complete the level and move on.

FAQ

Questions

Quick answers about TileBloom.

Is TileBloom free?

Yes — TileBloom is completely free to download and play. Earn coins in-game to use power-ups when you need them.

Does TileBloom need internet?

No. TileBloom works fully offline. All game progress is saved locally on your device — no Wi-Fi required.

How many levels are there?

TileBloom features 65+ hand-crafted levels across 5 themed worlds, with more being added in future updates.

What are the power-ups?

Undo reverses your last tile move. Shuffle rearranges all board tiles. Hint highlights a good tile to tap. Extra Slot gives you more space in the tray when things get tight.

Is there a time limit?

No. TileBloom is designed to be calming — take all the time you need. There's no clock, no pressure.
