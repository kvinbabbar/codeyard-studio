1. Who we are

TileBloom is published by Codeyard Studio ("we", "us"). Contact: codeyard.studio@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

TileBloom is a local-first game. We do not require you to create an account. Game progress — including completed levels, coins, settings preferences, and power-up inventory — is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifying information through the game itself.

3. Local storage

All game progress is stored on your device. If you uninstall TileBloom or clear the app's data, your progress will be permanently deleted from that device. We do not sync any game data to our servers.

4. Analytics & crash reporting

We may collect anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage analytics through Apple App Store or Google Play platform tools to improve the quality of TileBloom. This data is anonymised and not linked to you personally.

5. Advertising

The free version of TileBloom may show ads served by third-party ad networks. Ad networks may use device identifiers to deliver relevant advertisements, subject to your device privacy settings and, on iOS, the App Tracking Transparency framework.

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time.

6. In-app purchases

Coins and power-ups can be purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. All payment transactions are handled by the respective platform. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Purchase status may be stored locally on your device to unlock purchased content.

7. Device permissions

  • Haptics — used for tactile gameplay feedback only. No data is collected.
  • Audio — used for sound effects and music only. No data is collected.
  • Internet — required only for serving ads in the free version.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited technical data may be processed by Apple and Google for app distribution, crash diagnostics, in-app purchases, and ad delivery, according to their respective privacy policies.

9. Children

TileBloom is a game rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Your choices

  • Disable haptics or sound in the app Settings.
  • Limit ad tracking using your device's privacy settings.
  • Uninstall TileBloom to remove all locally stored game data from your device.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of TileBloom after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy: codeyard.studio@gmail.com.
