Privacy

Your business data is
nobody else's business.

We built Billcraft on a simple principle: your invoices, customers, and financial data belong to you — completely, forever. No accounts, no servers, no compromise.

  • No Cloud Sync

    All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Nothing leaves your phone — ever.

  • Zero Tracking

    We collect zero personal data. No analytics, no behavioral tracking, no third-party SDKs.

  • User-Controlled Backup

    Export and back up your data whenever you want. Backups are files you control — we never store them.

  • Fully Offline

    Every feature works without internet. Create invoices on a plane, in a café, or anywhere.

100% Local. 100% Yours.

Because we collect zero data, there's nothing to request, delete, or worry about. You have complete sovereignty over your business information.
