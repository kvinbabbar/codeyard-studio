FAQ

Questions

Quick answers about WeddWise.

Is my wedding data stored in the cloud?

No. WeddWise stores your wedding plans locally on your device using SQLite. You remain in control of your data.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Free planning is available with limits. Premium is a one-time lifetime unlock — no recurring fees.

Can I export my plans?

Lifetime Premium includes PDF wedding summary export and CSV import/export for guest lists.
