1. Who we are

Weddwise is published by Codeyard Studio (“we”, “us”). Contact: codeyard.studio@gmail.com.

2. What data we store

Weddwise is designed so your wedding content stays on your device. Typical information you enter includes couple names, dates, budget amounts, expenses, vendors, guests, RSVPs, tasks, invitation wording, and related notes.

We do not require you to create an account to use the core planning features.

3. Local storage

Wedding plans are stored locally using on-device database storage (SQLite). If you uninstall the app or clear app data, locally stored plans may be permanently deleted unless you previously exported them.

4. Purchases

Lifetime Premium is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Purchase status may be stored locally on your device to unlock premium features and restore purchases.

5. Advertising

The free version may show ads via Google AdMob. Ad networks may collect device identifiers and usage signals to deliver and measure ads, subject to your device settings and consent choices (including App Tracking Transparency on iOS where applicable).

Lifetime Premium removes advertisements.

6. Device permissions

  • Notifications — optional reminders for tasks and payments.
  • Photos / sharing — when you save or share invitation images or export files.
  • Tracking (iOS) — only if you allow it, related to personalized ads on the free plan.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your wedding content. Limited technical data may be processed by platform providers (Apple, Google) for distribution, crash diagnostics, ads (free plan), and in-app purchases according to their policies.

8. Children

Weddwise is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

9. Your choices

  • Edit or delete wedding data inside the app.
  • Disable notifications in system settings.
  • Upgrade to Lifetime Premium to remove ads.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data from the device.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: codeyard.studio@gmail.com.
