Common questions

I can’t find my wedding data after reinstalling.
Plans are stored on-device. Reinstalling without a backup/export can remove local data. Premium export features can help you keep a copy.

I bought Lifetime Premium but features are locked.
Open More → Premium (or the paywall) and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same App Store / Play account.

How do I turn off ads?
Upgrade to Lifetime Premium, which removes advertisements.

Reminders aren’t showing.
Enable notifications for Weddwise in system settings, then enable reminder options in the app (Premium).
