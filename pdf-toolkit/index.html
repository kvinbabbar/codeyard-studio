Features

Everything you need for your PDFs

A serious PDF workstation for professionals — library, edit, scan, organize, optimize, protect, and convert.

Local PDF Library

Import PDFs, organize with folders, favorites, and recent files, and search your documents — all stored on this device.

Edit & Annotate

Edit supported PDF text and embedded images, or annotate with highlights, drawings, notes, and signatures.

Scan & OCR

Capture documents with your camera, enhance pages, and run on-device OCR to create searchable PDFs where supported.

Organize Pages

Merge, split, extract, reorder, rotate, delete, and duplicate pages without rebuilding documents from scratch.

Optimize PDFs

Structural optimization preserves text and vectors whenever possible — with flatten & compress when you need maximum size reduction.

Redact & Sanitize

Remove sensitive content with structural redaction where supported, and clean metadata and other sensitive structures before sharing.

Professional Tools

Compare PDFs, Bates numbering, supported forms, repair analysis, PDF/A status checks, and certificate inspection.

Convert & Finish

Convert to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, and text. Add watermarks, page numbers, headers, and crop pages.

Privacy

Your PDFs stay
on your device.

We built PDF Toolkit for professionals who need real PDF power without sending documents to a cloud converter. Core operations run locally whenever supported.

  • Local-first library

    Your PDF library and document index live on device. Core viewing, editing, OCR, and conversion do not upload file contents for processing.

  • No analytics SDK

    PDF Toolkit does not ship product analytics. Purchases may use RevenueCat for entitlements — which never receives your PDF contents.

  • User-controlled backup

    Create local backups you control. We do not host or sync your backup folders to Codeyard Studio servers.

  • Works offline

    Core PDF tools work without internet. Store listings and purchase restoration may require connectivity when you choose to use them.

On-device. Under your control.

Your documents are not sent to Codeyard Studio for processing. You decide what to share from the system share sheet.
