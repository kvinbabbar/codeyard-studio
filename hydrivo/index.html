Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hydrivo's features, sync, and privacy.

Is Hydrivo free to use?
Yes! Hydrivo is 100% free to download. Core tracking, daily goal calculations, reminders, beverage logging, statistics, and Apple Health / Health Connect sync are completely free. An optional Pro upgrade is available for users who want an ad-free experience, custom drink definitions, and advanced widget themes.
How does Apple Health & Google Health Connect sync work?
Hydrivo requests read/write access to Dietary Water in Apple Health (iOS) and Google Health Connect (Android). Whenever you record a drink in Hydrivo, it automatically logs the calculated water volume into your health dashboard so your fitness and nutrition metrics remain in sync.
Can I log drinks besides plain water?
Yes. Hydrivo includes presets for coffee, tea, fruit juice, milk, sports drinks, soda, beer, wine, and liquor. Each beverage includes a hydration coefficient (e.g. coffee counts for ~80% net hydration, sports drinks count for 100%) to give you an accurate picture of your true hydration status.
Is my personal data private and secure?
Yes, absolutely. Hydrivo does not require any account, email, or login. All your hydration logs, daily targets, and streak histories are saved strictly in your phone's local storage. We do not sell or collect your health information.
Will reminders wake me up during the night?
No. In Hydrivo Settings, you configure your typical wake-up and bed times. Notifications are intelligently scheduled only during your active awake hours so your rest is never disturbed.
