Common Help Topics

How do I enable Apple Health or Google Health Connect?
Inside Hydrivo, open Settings and locate the Health Sync section. Toggle on Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). Your device will present a system authorization prompt. Ensure that "Dietary Water" (both Read and Write) is enabled.
Why aren't my hydration reminders appearing?
Please verify the following:
  • Go to your phone's system Settings > Notifications > Hydrivo and ensure Allow Notifications is enabled.
  • In Hydrivo Settings > Reminders, verify that your "Wake Time" and "Bed Time" accurately reflect your waking hours. Hydrivo automatically silences notifications during sleep hours.
  • Check that your device is not in Do Not Disturb / Focus mode.
How do I add the Hydrivo Home Screen Widget?
On iOS: Long-press any empty space on your Home Screen until apps jiggle. Tap the + button in the top left, search for Hydrivo, choose your preferred widget size (Small or Medium), and tap Add Widget.

On Android: Touch and hold an empty space on your Home Screen, tap Widgets, find Hydrivo, and drag the widget to your desired position.
How do I restore my Hydrivo Pro purchase?
If you switched devices or reinstalled the app, navigate to Settings > Hydrivo Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase will automatically be verified with your Apple ID or Google account at no charge.
How do I clear or reset my hydration history?
In Hydrivo, tap Settings > Reset Data. You will be prompted to confirm. Doing so deletes all logged drinks, streaks, and custom settings from your device.
