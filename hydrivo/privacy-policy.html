Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 · Hydrivo by Codeyard Studio

Privacy Summary: Hydrivo is built with a strict privacy-first principle. We do not require account creation, emails, or passwords. Your hydration logs, body metrics, goals, and history are stored 100% locally on your personal device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your health data.

1. Introduction

Codeyard Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates Hydrivo – Water Tracker & Reminder (the "Application" or "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information, how third-party frameworks operate, and what choices you have regarding your privacy.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Hydrivo stores the following information strictly on your local device:

  • Daily Hydration Target: Your target goal in milliliters or fluid ounces.
  • Drink Logs: Timestamps, beverage types (water, coffee, tea, etc.), and volumes consumed.
  • Custom Preferences: Reminder intervals, quiet hours, preferred units (ml / fl oz), and container sizes.
  • User Profile: Approximate body weight, activity level, and climate (used locally solely to calculate suggested daily hydration targets).
  • Streaks & Achievements: Milestones and badges unlocked over time.

None of this information is transmitted to or stored on Codeyard Studio remote servers.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit) Integration

On iOS devices, Hydrivo can optionally integrate with Apple HealthKit. If you grant explicit permission:

  • Writing Hydration Data: When you record drinks in Hydrivo, the app records corresponding Dietary Water entries in HealthKit.
  • Reading Hydration Data: Hydrivo may read Dietary Water entries created today to ensure your intake progress is synchronized if logged through other apps or Apple Watch.

Strict HealthKit Protections: In compliance with Apple developer guidelines, HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, data mining, or shared with third parties. You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time via iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Hydrivo.

4. Google Health Connect Integration

On Android devices, Hydrivo can optionally integrate with Google Health Connect. If you grant permission:

  • Hydrivo writes and synchronizes your daily water consumption records with Health Connect.
  • Health Connect data is handled in strict compliance with the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Health Connect data is never used for marketing, advertising, or sold to third parties.

5. Notifications & Reminders

Hydrivo uses device-level local notification scheduling to deliver reminders during your active daytime hours. Reminders are generated entirely by the operating system on your phone; no external push notification service is used to track your habits. You can customize intervals or disable notifications entirely in the App Settings or system preferences.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier of Hydrivo displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

  • On iOS, Hydrivo respects the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you select "Ask App not to Track", non-personalized ads are served.
  • You may permanently remove all advertising by upgrading to Hydrivo Pro via an in-app purchase.

7. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

If you purchase Hydrivo Pro (lifetime unlock or subscription), the transaction is processed securely through Apple App Store (Apple Inc.) or Google Play Store (Google LLC). We do not collect or store your credit card, billing address, or payment details.

8. Children's Privacy

Hydrivo does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian discovers that an unauthorized submission has occurred, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights & Data Deletion (Reset Data)

Because your data is held strictly on your device:

  • You have complete control over your records at all times.
  • You can wipe all drink logs, streaks, and settings in one tap by going to Settings > Reset Data within Hydrivo.
  • Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally cached databases and preferences from your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
